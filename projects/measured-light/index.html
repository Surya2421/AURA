[OPTICAL PHILOSOPHY // METHODOLOGY]

The Camera as an Instrument of Measurement

Architectural photography disciplined by solar geometry, tectonic mass, and optical perspective rectification.

01

The Solar Ephemeris

Architecture is rendered visible solely by natural solar incidence. The studio conducts preliminary ephemeris calculations to chart sun azimuth and altitude angles across equinoxes, documenting structures precisely when raking light isolates surface texture and volumetric depth.

[SOLAR INCIDENCE DISCIPLINE]
02

Tectonic Mass & Cast Void

Rather than producing superficial marketing impressions, our compositions isolate structural load-bearing mass, cantilevered canopies, and cast negative voids. Shadow is recorded as a sculptured physical substance rather than an absence of information.

[SPATIAL VOID MEASUREMENT]
03

Large-Format Optical Rigor

Employing calibrated perspective-control optics and large-format sensor discipline, every vertical axis remains strictly parallel with zero optical keystoning. The resulting monographs honor the tectonic precision envisioned by the architect and engineer.

[PERSPECTIVE RECTIFICATION]
